Manage user access

Admin, Chef 1, and Chef 2 are fixed accounts. You can enable or disable the two manager accounts here — anyone signing in with a disabled account will fall back to Viewer. Usernames and passwords cannot be changed from this screen.

Master Archive

Save a snapshot of every module (recipe cards, production plans, packages, inventory, and stock history) as a downloadable Excel workbook. Useful before switching to a new month of planning — previous snapshots stay listed here for re-download.
